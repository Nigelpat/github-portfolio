</div>
<div id="a2" class="answer">
There are provided garden rotarys and hoes in the shed.
</div>

<div class="question" onclick="toggleAnswer('a3')">
Are tools provided?
</div>
<div id="a3" class="answer">
Basic gardening tools such as shovels, rakes, rotarys, hoes, and watering cans are
available for community use. However, gardeners are encouraged to bring
their own gloves and personal tools if they prefer. All shared tools should
be cleaned and returned after use so everyone can benefit from them.
</div>

<div class="question" onclick="toggleAnswer('a4')">
What are the best plants for each season?
</div>
<div id="a4" class="answer">
Plant choices depend on the season. In spring, popular plants include lettuce,
spinach, carrots, and peas. Summer is ideal for tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers,
and squash. In the fall, many gardeners grow broccoli, kale, and cabbage.
</div>

<div class="question" onclick="toggleAnswer('a5')">
How do I apply for a garden plot?
</div>
<div id="a5" class="answer">
To apply for a garden plot, create an account and submit a lease application through
the "Plot" page.
</div>

<div class="question" onclick="toggleAnswer('a6')">
What happens if I cannot maintain my plot for a period of time?
</div>
<div id="a6" class="answer">
If you are unable to care for your plot temporarily, you can schedule a caretaker
using the garden calendar or notify the garden administrator.
</div>
