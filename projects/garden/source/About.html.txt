   <h2>About Our Garden</h2>
<p>
The Community Garden Management System was created to help organize and support
local gardeners who share a passion for growing food, flowers, and community.
Our goal is to provide an easy way for gardeners and administrators to manage
garden plots, schedule garden use, communicate with members, and keep the
garden thriving.
</p>

<h2>Our Mission</h2>
<p>
Our mission is to promote sustainable gardening, community engagement, and
environmental awareness. By bringing people together through gardening,
we help build stronger neighborhoods and encourage healthy living.
</p>

<h2>What This System Provides</h2>
<ul>
<li>Create and manage gardener accounts</li>
<li>Submit lease applications for garden plots</li>
<li>Priority-based plot assignment</li>
<li>Calendar scheduling for garden access and caretaking</li>
<li>Online payment tracking for plot leases</li>
<li>Email communication between gardeners and administrators</li>
<li>Question and answer forms to gather gardener feedback</li>
<li>Interactive garden layout and plot management</li>
</ul>

<h2>Get Involved</h2>
<p>
Whether you are an experienced gardener or just getting started, our
community garden welcomes everyone. Apply for a plot, volunteer, or
participate in community gardening events to help make our garden grow.
</p>
